Job description

Hiring: Assistant Professors For All Departments:


Experience: 0-2 years
Eligibility: Relevant postgraduate qualification in the respective discipline

Job Location: Thiruvallur, Chennai
Suitable for: Freshers and early-career teaching professionals


Prathyusha Engineering College | Autonomous Institution

Prathyusha Engineering College invites applications from enthusiastic and dedicated postgraduate candidates for Assistant Professor positions across Engineering, Technology, Management, and Science & Humanities departments.

Departments: AI & Data Science, AI & Machine Learning, Computer Science & Engineering, Computer Science & Business Systems, Cyber Security, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Biotechnology, MBA, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and English.

Role & Responsibilities

  • Deliver engaging lectures, tutorials and practical sessions in the relevant subject.
  • Prepare lesson plans, presentations, course materials and assessments.
  • Conduct laboratory sessions, demonstrations, assignments and project guidance as applicable.
  • Mentor students in academic performance, technical skills and career development.
  • Support student projects, workshops, seminars, technical events and departmental activities.
  • Assist with examination duties, evaluation, academic documentation and outcome-based education (OBE) processes.
  • Participate in departmental meetings, curriculum-related activities, faculty development programmes and accreditation documentation.
  • Encourage innovation, research, publications and industry-oriented learning.
  • Integrate relevant digital tools and emerging technologies into teaching and learning.

Candidates should possess strong subject knowledge, effective communication skills and a passion for teaching, student mentoring and academic development.


Salary: As per qualification, experience and institutional norms.


Interested candidates may apply with an updated CV or Send your resume to hr@prathyusha.edu.in

About company

Prathyusha Engineering College, Chennai popularly known as PEC is a self-financed Telugu Minority Institution managed by Prathyusha Educational Trust,Chennai,approved by AICTE, affiliated to Anna University, with accredited by NBA Programme and a recipient of ISO certification, located 30 kms from Chennai Central Railway Station. The college was conferred with autonomous status from the year 2023-2024.The college was established in the year 2001, to promote engineering education with quality technical knowledge to all the students hailing from not only in and around Chennai, but from various districts in Tamilnadu and from neighbouring states also. This elysium of education, is the vision of the Chairman Shri. P. Raja Rao, the well-known industrialist and philanthropist, to bring world-class education into this sylvan campus.
